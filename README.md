## Books

- [The Manager's Path](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491975880/) - Practical guide for new tech managers and leaders.
- [Blue Ocean Strategy](https://www.blueoceanstrategy.com/) - Framework for creating uncontested market space.
- [How to Start a Startup](https://www.startupclass.samaltman.com/) - Free course and guide from Sam Altman and Y Combinator.
- [System Design Interview](https://www.amazon.com/System-Design-Interview-insiders-Second/dp/1736049119) - Guide to designing large-scale systems.
- [Building LLMs for Production](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/building-llms-for/9781098150955/) - Practical guide for deploying language models at scale.
- [Moral Mazes: The World of Corporate Managers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_Mazes) - Sociology classic on how bureaucracy shapes how managers think and act.
- [Prototype to Product](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/prototype-to-product/9781449362959/) - A practical workflow for taking software from idea to shipped product.
- [Quantitative Trading](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/quantitative-trading-how-to-build-your-own-algorithmic-trading-business-2nd-edition-p-9781119800071) - Ernest Chan's guide to building your own algorithmic trading business.
- [The Sense of Style](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310859/the-sense-of-style-by-steven-pinker/) - Steven Pinker's thinking person's guide to writing well.
- [The Definitive Book of Body Language](https://www.orionbooks.co.uk/titles/allan-pease/the-definitive-book-of-body-language/9781399636636/) - Reading and using nonverbal communication in business.
- [Modern Manners](https://www.amazon.com/Modern-Manners-Tools-Take-You/dp/0770434088) - Dorothea Johnson and Liv Tyler on professional etiquette.
- [Watching the English](https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/kate-fox/watching-the-english/9781529349757/) - Kate Fox's anthropological guide to English social rules.
- [Blockchain Chicken Farm](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780374538668/blockchainchickenfarm/) - On tech, rural China, and what technological progress does to ordinary people.

## Scheduling & Communication

- [Calendly](https://calendly.com) - Scheduling automation for meetings and appointments.
- [Zcal](https://zcal.com) - Visual scheduling platform for teams.
- [Zero Calendar](https://www.zerocalendar.com) - AI-powered scheduling assistant for appointment coordination.
- [Slack](https://slack.com) - Team messaging and collaboration hub.
- [Signal](https://signal.org) - Secure, encrypted messaging for sensitive business conversations.
- [Missive](https://missiveapp.com) - Collaborative team inbox combining email, chat, and tasks.

## Business Phone Systems

- [Quo (formerly OpenPhone)](https://www.quo.com/) - Business phone system built for startups, with shared numbers, SMS, and automations.
- [Dialpad](https://www.dialpad.com/) - AI-powered business phone and contact center platform.
- [Aircall](https://aircall.io/) - Cloud-based call center and phone system for sales and support teams.
- [Mint Mobile](https://www.mintmobile.com) - Budget prepaid wireless plans for keeping business and personal lines cheap.

## Project & Team Management

- [Trello](https://trello.com) - Visual project management with boards, lists, and cards.
- [ClickUp](https://clickup.com) - All-in-one productivity platform for tasks, docs, and goals.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.so) - Connected workspace for notes, docs, wikis, and project management.
- [Asana](https://asana.com) - Work management for tracking projects, tasks, and goals across teams.
- [Linear](https://linear.app) - Streamlined issue tracking for modern software teams.
- [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com/) - Simple, cross-platform task manager with natural-language input.
- [Affine](https://affine.pro/) - Open-source knowledge base and workspace for docs, whiteboards, and databases.
- [Zapier](https://zapier.com) - No-code automation connecting your apps and workflows.
- [Make](https://www.make.com/) - Visual automation platform for building complex integrations.
- [n8n](https://n8n.io) - Open-source workflow automation with self-hosting options.
- [Retool](https://retool.com) - Build internal tools and admin panels fast.

## Sales, CRM & Outreach

- [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io) - All-in-one sales intelligence and engagement platform.
- [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io) - Email finding and verification service.
- [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com) - Sales CRM built around a visual pipeline for deal tracking.
- [Attio](https://attio.com/) - Modern, flexible CRM for fast-growing teams.
- [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com) - Audience research tool that finds what your customers read, watch, and follow.
- [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com) - Competitive and market intelligence on website traffic and audience.

## Virtual Address & Mailbox

- [Anytime Mailbox](https://www.anytimemailbox.com/) - Virtual business addresses with mail scanning and forwarding.
- [Stable](https://www.usestable.com/) - Mailbox and address management for businesses and individuals.

## Ecommerce & Dropshipping

- [DSers](https://www.dsers.com) - Dropshipping order automation tool for AliExpress and Shopify.
- [Spocket](https://www.spocket.co) - Dropshipping marketplace of fast-shipping, mostly US/EU suppliers.
- [QR Code Generator](https://the-qrcode-generator.com) - Free tool for generating QR codes for products and packaging.
- [How to Make NFC Amiibo Cards](https://electronics.alibaba.com/buyingguides/how-to-make-nfc-amiibo-cards-a-practical-2026-guide) - Practical guide to producing NFC Amiibo cards as a product line.

## Banking, Finance & Bookkeeping

- [Mercury](https://mercury.com/) - Online banking built for startups and founders.
- [Brex](https://www.brex.com/) - Business accounts, corporate cards, and spend management for startups.
- [Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas) - Incorporate a US company and open a bank account remotely.
- [Xero](https://www.xero.com) - Cloud accounting software for small businesses.
- [QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com) - Bookkeeping, invoicing, and payroll for small business.
- [Pilot](https://pilot.com/) - Bookkeeping, tax, and CFO services for startups.
- [Puzzle](https://puzzle.io/) - Modern accounting software for fast-growing businesses.
- [Relay](https://www.relaypayments.com/) - Digital payments for logistics and trucking operations.
- [LegalZoom](https://www.legalzoom.com) - Legal documents, LLC formation, and business compliance services.
